About the Hackathon
INITIATIVE IS LIVE
Where Banking Meets The Future Of FinTech
IDBI Innovate 2026 is a national-level innovation challenge that brings together the brightest minds in India's Startup and FinTech ecosystem to solve real-world banking problems — and build solutions scalable enough for deployment within IDBI Bank.

This isn't a sandbox exercise. Selected solutions will follow a structured roadmap, with the best teams earning a direct path for submitting a Proof of Concept within the bank's sandbox environment.

IDBI Bank shall provide shortlisted participants/teams with sandbox APIs, synthetic banking datasets, cloud infrastructure, and direct access to mentors and digital transformation experts throughout the challenge.

01 — AI & Data Analytics at the Core
Solutions must leverage artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data-driven methodologies to address banking challenges at scale.

02 — Real Problems, Real Stakes
Each problem statement is sourced directly from IDBI Bank's business units — addressing operational gaps with measurable business impact.

03 — Open to Professionals, Startups & FinTechs
Whether you're an early-stage startup or an established FinTech, IDBI Innovate is designed to accommodate teams at every stage of maturity.

04 — Prizes and Rewards
Beyond rewards, winning teams may unlock a structured pathway towards co-development and implementation roadmap with IDBI Bank.

Challenges — Four Problem Statements & An Open Runway To Redefine Banking

TRACK 01 — Wealth Advisory · Conversational AI · Mobile Banking
PROBLEM STATEMENT: Wealth management and advisory services remain fragmented and largely inaccessible to large number of customers. Absence of comprehensive, customer investment behaviour and spending habits limits the ability to provide timely, personalized, data-driven guidance.
EXPECTED OUTCOME: Bank is looking for AI powered Digital Wealth management (Avatar Based) Application which integrates into the bank's mobile application, to deliver personalized and scalable wealth advisory services through an intuitive digital interface.

TRACK 02 — Lead Generation · Behavioural Analytics · Retail Lending
PROBLEM STATEMENT: Bank's retail lending relies on traditional metrics, resulting in low conversions and limited insight into customer intent. A data-driven approach is needed to identify eligible, quantifiable repayment capacity, genuinely interested prospects using transaction and behavioral insights.
EXPECTED OUTCOME: The solution is expected to generate high-quality leads with a conversion rate exceeding 30%, while also enabling accurate assessment of borrowers' actual income levels to support prudent underwriting for loans such as Personal Loan, Home loan, Mortgage Loan, Auto Loan.

TRACK 03 — Financial Inclusion · Digital Lending · Credit Decisioning
PROBLEM STATEMENT: Bank's MSME credit evaluation relies on traditional financial documents, which many New-to-Credit (NTC) and New-to-Bank (NTB) enterprises lack or maintain inadequately. Despite availability of rich alternate data (GST, UPI, AA, EPFO, etc.), absence of a unified assessment framework leads to high rejection rates, missed viable borrowers, limited portfolio diversification, and slower financial inclusion progress.
EXPECTED OUTCOME: Participants should design an AI/ML-driven MSME Financial Health Card that aggregates alternate data (GST, UPI, AA, EPFO, etc.), computes a multidimensional financial health score, visualizes strengths and risks, integrates with ULI/OCEN/AA ecosystems, enables near real-time credit assessment, and expands onboarding of credit-invisible MSMEs while improving portfolio quality.

TRACK 04 — MSME Credit · Predictive AI · Risk Management
PROBLEM STATEMENT: The current capability is limited by low prediction accuracy in the range of 16 - 22%, dependent solely on structured data and fragmented methodologies across loan types and borrower segments.
EXPECTED OUTCOME: The expected outcome is to develop a robust predictive solution that estimates the probability of default to identify potential stress in loans 12 months in advance, with accuracy improving to 90%. The solution should use both structured and unstructured data, apply suitable analytical methods for different loan types and borrower profiles and provide a common interpretation framework to ensure consistent, comparable and actionable outputs across all MSME loans.

WILDCARD OPEN TRACK 05 — WINNER PRIZE: ₹1,00,000 · Disruptive Technology · Open Innovation · Industry Transformation
THE RUNWAY MAP: Do you have a groundbreaking blueprint that sits completely outside our defined parameters? This open innovation track is created explicitly for visionaries, professionals, and FinTechs with a completely original concept, innovative business model, or software architecture capable of fundamentally transforming the banking landscape.
EXPECTED TECHNICAL IMPACT: We welcome completely out-of-the-box submissions—ranging from decentralized identity systems and decentralized ledgers to next-gen security fabrics or omni-channel customer experiences. Present a high-performance solution that demonstrates long-term commercial scalability and real integration viability inside IDBI Bank's ecosystem.

Rewards & Recognition — More Than A Prize. An Opportunity.
TOTAL PRIZE POOL: ₹15,00,000 — Distributed across all tracks

Prize Distribution Architecture
- Problem Statements (Tracks 01-04): Each track awards one winner and one runner-up. ₹2 Lakhs for Winner, ₹1 Lakh for Runner-up.
- Novel Idea Track (Track 05): Features a standalone top innovation prize. ₹1 Lakh for Track Winner.

Strict Participation Directives
! Multiple track submissions: If you have solutions for more than one theme, you don't have to choose just one—teams are officially permitted to make multiple submissions across different problem statements to maximize their chances!
! Originality Requirement: Submissions targeting the Novel Idea track must be completely distinct from the core themes, methodologies, or objectives of the 4 defined problem statements.

Talent Pipeline: IDBI Bank may engage directly with the winning teams.
Implementation: Top POCs may gain access to the Bank's sandbox environment leading to POC within IDBI Bank.
Senior Mentorship: Finalists receive intensive guidance from Bank's Senior Management and Digital Transformation Leaders.
Collaboration: Mature Startups and high-quality FinTechs and experienced professionals may be invited into formal collaboration with the bank.
